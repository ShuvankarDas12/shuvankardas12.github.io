About

Professional summary

I am a scholarship-funded Mechatronics and Robotics engineer with an MSc from the University of Leeds and a B.Tech in Mechatronics Engineering from KIIT. I work across the full engineering workflow: firmware development on Arduino, ESP32 and NVIDIA Jetson, real-time control and sensor integration with EMG, IMU, LiDAR and depth cameras, structural simulation with ANSYS FEA and CFD, and CAD in SolidWorks and Catia V5. I also have hands-on experience of PLC and SCADA-based industrial automation.

My projects span medical devices, UAVs and manufacturing, and I judge them by measured results. These include a 46% reduction in bill-of-materials cost, EMG control latency below 100 ms, 30% fewer clinical visits through design innovation and a 50% reduction in operator intervention time. I have designed to the ISO 13485 medical device standard, applied Poka-Yoke quality systems on an automotive assembly line, worked through Agile and iterative prototyping, and developed vehicles through Formula Student projects.

I am seeking roles in robotics engineering, wearable robotics, embedded systems, automation, UAV systems and medical device development.
